# Hillshade interpretation and sensitivity

Hillshade is a visualization derived from a surface and illumination settings. It is useful for interpreting relief but is not itself elevation, slope, stability, or accessibility.

A later sensitivity check should hold the elevation surface constant while varying azimuth and altitude, then explain which apparent features move with illumination. Record the z-factor and map projection. Avoid interpreting a dark area as a physical hazard without supporting terrain or field evidence.
